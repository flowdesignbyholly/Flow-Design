When hired as a Live Painter, I attend your event and paint you and your guests live. For more information or to discuss me attending your event, contact me at flowdesignbyholly@gmail.com or on Instagram @flowdesignbyholly

How Live Painting Works

I take quick photographs of guests to sketch from - working from photographs means that guests can continue to enjoy the event whilst I paint! At the end of the event, guests collect their paintings and I provide envelopes for safe keeping, or a frame (frames can be purchased in advance within the booking or be made available to guests to buy on the day). I create a display of paintings as I complete them so that guests can enjoy them throughout the event.

I paint approximately 10 guests per hour, meaning I will paint approximately 40 guests (4 hours), 60 guests (6 hours), or 80 guests (8 hours). Most paintings are done in pairs, but I am more than happy to accommodate individuals and groups (and the occasional dog!).

I paint at a time that best suits your occasion and arrive ahead of my painting time to set up.
