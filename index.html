Welcome to Flow Design! I'm Holly, a UK Based Artist and Illustrator capturing memories in paint.

I love spreading joy by recreating special photographs as artwork or personalised cards, and painting guests live at weddings and events. I predominately use watercolour painting and linework, creating unique and charming artworks with a personal touch.

Personalised cards are available via my website or to discuss commissioned artwork or live painting then do get in touch via email at flowdesignbyholly@gmail.com - I look forward to the posibility of painting for you!
